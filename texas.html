About Texas LPC Jurisprudence

The Texas LPC Jurisprudence Examination is required for licensure through the Texas Behavioral Health Executive Council. It tests knowledge of the Texas Licensed Professional Counselor Act, Council rules, and related Texas law that governs professional counseling practice in the state.

Typical study time: Most candidates spend 1 to 2 weeks. The exam is focused on Texas statutes and rules, so a concentrated review of the LPC Act and Council regulations over 7–14 days is typical.

Content centers on scope of practice, ethical and legal duties, reporting requirements, supervision, and other Texas-specific professional standards.
