About Me

I am Jesse Johnson, a U.S. Army Combat Veteran of Operation Iraqi Freedom (2008 to 2009) with 19 years of professional experience in technology. I have served and currently work for the U.S. Department of War and hold the CompTIA Security+ certification.

As a lifelong learner, I am an alumnus of Southern Nazarene University (Class of 2020, Master's in Business) and Mid-America Christian University (Class of 2027, Master's in Clinical Mental Health Counseling, CMHC). I built this platform to keep my study materials structured and accessible, and I hope it helps you reach your goals as well.
