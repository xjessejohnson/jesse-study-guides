About OLERE

The Oklahoma Legal and Ethical Responsibilities Examination (OLERE) tests state-specific knowledge required for LPC licensure in Oklahoma. It covers the Oklahoma Licensed Professional Counselor Act, administrative rules, ethics, mandated reporting, scope of practice, and continuing education requirements.

Typical study time: Most candidates spend 1 to 2 weeks of focused review. Because the material is narrower and state-specific, intensive study over 7–14 days is usually sufficient if you already know general counseling ethics.

The exam focuses on Oklahoma statutes and rules rather than broad counseling theory. Key sources include Title 59 (LPC Act) and OAC 86:10, along with ACA ethics as they apply in Oklahoma.

LPC Act Title 59
Rules OAC 86:10
Ethics ACA + OK
Reporting Mandated

Use the practice exams, flashcards, and sprint drills here to lock in the Oklahoma-specific rules and ethics.

Roles this supports: Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC) in Oklahoma. Passing the OLERE is one step toward Oklahoma LPC licensure, which allows independent counseling practice under state rules.

Typical salaries (as of 11/26/2026): Once licensed, Oklahoma LPCs fall under the broader mental health counselor category. BLS median for substance abuse, behavioral disorder, and mental health counselors is about $59,350 nationally (May 2025). State and private-practice earnings vary; check current Oklahoma wage data for local figures.
