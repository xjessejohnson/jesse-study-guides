OLERE
(OKLAHOMA)

The Oklahoma Legal and Ethical Responsibilities Examination (OLERE) tests knowledge of state-specific counseling laws, LPC regulations, and ethical standards required for licensure in Oklahoma. Content covers the Oklahoma Licensed Professional Counselor Act, administrative rules, mandated reporting, scope of practice, and continuing education requirements.
