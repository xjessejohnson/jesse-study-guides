About CEH

The Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) from EC-Council validates knowledge of ethical hacking tools, techniques, and methodologies. It is oriented toward understanding how attackers operate so you can better defend systems.

Typical study time: Most candidates spend 6 to 10 weeks. Those with strong networking or penetration-testing backgrounds may need less; beginners should plan closer to 8–12 weeks.

The exam is multiple-choice and covers the phases of ethical hacking, common tools, and defensive countermeasures. Content is organized around the attack lifecycle and related technical domains.

Common roles after CEH: Security Analyst, Penetration Tester, Vulnerability Analyst, Security Consultant, Red Team Operator (often with additional certs).

Typical US salaries (as of 11/26/2026): Security Analyst $75k–$110k; Penetration Tester $96k–$141k; Red Team Operator $120k–$165k. PayScale reports a CEH median base around $96k; other surveys show higher averages depending on role and experience. Hands-on skills and additional certifications strongly affect pay.
