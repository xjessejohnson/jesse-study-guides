The National Counselor Examination (NCE) is administered by the National Board for Certified Counselors (NBCC) for counselor licensure and national certification, featuring 200 multiple-choice questions (160 scored and 40 unscored pilot items) with a typical passing cut score of 95 to 105 out of 160 (60% to 66%), or a 150+ out of 200 (75%+) target on this full practice test.
