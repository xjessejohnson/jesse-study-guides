STUDY HUB

The Certified Information Security Manager (CISM) is an executive management credential issued by ISACA. It consists of 150 multiple-choice questions scored on a 200 to 800 scale (450+ required to pass) across four domains focused on enterprise governance, risk management, program development, and incident response.

1. Information Security Governance 17%
2. Information Security Risk Management 20%
3. Information Security Program 33%
4. Incident Management 30%
